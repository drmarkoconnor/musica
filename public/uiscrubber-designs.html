UI exploration

Lesson audio segment chooser

Selected direction: audio-first chapter rail for long lesson navigation, wired to a created-clip review queue with editable titles and notes.

Branch uiscrubber

What works now

  • One custom timeline owns playback, playhead, clip preview, and 20 second capture.
  • Selected range, existing clips, and audio rows are visible in the same workflow.
  • Start and end can be set by playhead, slider, or fine one second nudge.

Main friction

  • The waveform is decorative rather than derived from the recording.
  • Whole-lesson scale makes long recordings hard to trim precisely.
  • Timeline scrubbing, range handles, and saved clips are visually separate decisions.

Design target

  • Keep dense teacher-review workflow, not a marketing-style media player.
  • Use larger hit areas, zoom or focus range, and stronger selected-clip contrast.
  • Make saved segments feel like first-class markers on the recording.

Chosen direction

Use Option 3 as the audio-first lesson map before transcription. Pair it with Option 5 for the clips that have already been created: saved clips appear in a review queue, selecting a marker in the rail selects the same clip in the queue, and the queue owns title and note editing. Transcript-led selection stays deferred until after authorised transcription exists.
